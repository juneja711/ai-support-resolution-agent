# NovaTech Shipping and Delivery Policy

1. Shipping Methods & Delivery Times
- Standard Shipping: Delivery takes between 3 to 7 business days following dispatch.
- Expedited Shipping: Delivery takes between 1 to 2 business days following dispatch.
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday are processed and dispatched on the same business day.

2. Shipping Rates
- Orders with a subtotal of $50.00 or more qualify for Free Standard Shipping across the continental US.
- Standard shipping for orders under $50.00 is a flat rate of $4.99.
- Expedited 2-Day Shipping is available at checkout for $14.99.

3. Tracking Your Order
- A shipping confirmation email with a live tracking number is sent automatically as soon as your order leaves our warehouse.
- You can also check real-time order status directly through our AI Support Assistant by providing your Order ID.

4. Damaged or Lost Shipments
- If your package arrives damaged or does not arrive within 10 business days of the shipping date, please notify customer support to open an investigation and request a replacement or refund.
