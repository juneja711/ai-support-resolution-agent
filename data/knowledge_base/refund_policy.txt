# NovaTech Customer Refund Policy

1. Overview
At NovaTech, customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, you may request a refund under the conditions described below.

2. Eligibility Window
- Customers are eligible to request a refund within 30 calendar days from the date the order is marked as 'Delivered'.
- Products that have not been delivered cannot be refunded directly; they must either be cancelled (if still processing) or returned after delivery.

3. Condition Requirements
- Items must be in their original condition, unworn or unused, with all original tags, manuals, and accessories included.
- Items damaged due to customer misuse, liquid damage, or accidental drops are not eligible for a refund.
- Digital downloads, opened software, and customized products are strictly non-refundable.

4. Refund Process and Timeline
- Once an eligible refund request is approved, our finance department initiates the refund to the original method of payment.
- Refunds typically take between 5 to 7 business days to reflect on your credit card or bank account statement, depending on your financial institution.
- Shipping fees paid on the original order are non-refundable unless the return was due to our company's error.
