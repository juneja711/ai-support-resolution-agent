# NovaTech Customer Return Policy

1. Return Window
- Returns must be initiated within 30 days of product delivery.
- Products delivered beyond 30 days cannot be accepted for return.

2. Return Conditions & Packaging
- Items must be returned in their original packaging, including protective foam, cables, manuals, and warranty cards.
- Please do not apply tape or shipping labels directly onto the manufacturer retail packaging; place the product inside an outer shipping box.

3. Return Shipping Costs
- If an item is returned due to defect, transit damage, or shipping error, NovaTech provides a prepaid return shipping label free of charge.
- For voluntary returns (e.g., change of mind, incorrect size/model ordered), the customer is responsible for the return postage cost.

4. Inspection and Approval
- Returned packages are inspected within 2 to 3 business days of arrival at our central fulfillment center.
- Upon inspection approval, the return status will update, and a refund or exchange will be issued immediately.
